In recent years, remote work has moved from being a novelty to becoming a norm for many companies around the world. Technological advances, high-speed internet, video conferencing tools, and cloud-based software have made it possible for employees to collaborate effectively without being physically present in the same office. However, with these benefits come challenges, maintaining team cohesion, ensuring work-life balance, and avoiding feelings of isolation among workers, employers and employees alike are still learning how to adapt policies and habits to make remote work productive and sustainable over time.
